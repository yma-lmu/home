Explore research, summer programs, and mathematical communities. Check each program’s website for its current dates, eligibility, and application instructions.

Research at LMU

  • Summer Opportunities for Advanced Research (SOAR)

    Faculty propose projects and nominate students. Speak with a faculty mentor about participating.

  • Coastal Research Institute opportunities

    Student internships and research opportunities with CRI.

  • Summer Undergraduate Research Program (SURP)

    Support for undergraduate research and creative work. The former Independent Undergraduate Research Program link now leads to SURP.

  • Research Learning Community (RLC)
  • Rains Research Assistant Program

    Applications are initiated by faculty members.

Programs beyond LMU

  • NSF Research Experiences for Undergraduates

    Find NSF-funded research opportunities and apply directly to individual programs.

  • Mathematics REU directory

    An additional directory of undergraduate research programs in mathematics.

  • EDGE Summer Program

    A summer program supporting women and gender-nonconforming students preparing for or beginning doctoral study in the mathematical sciences.

  • Smith College Postbaccalaureate Program in Mathematics

    Advanced undergraduate mathematics preparation for bachelor’s-degree holders seeking graduate study. See the program website for prerequisites and eligibility.

Conferences, mentoring & funding

  • Nebraska Conference for Undergraduate Wisdom in Mathematics

    An undergraduate mathematics conference featuring student research, mentoring, graduate-school guidance, and career exploration. Formerly the Nebraska Conference for Undergraduate Women in Mathematics.

  • AAUW fellowships and grants

    Graduate fellowships and other funding opportunities. Eligibility varies by award.

  • Association for Women in Mathematics: undergraduate resources
  • AWM resources on women in mathematics

    Reading, history, role models, and further resources.
